## Supplementary Methods

### Sensitivity Analysis Details

#### Age Sensitivity Analyses

**1. Exclusion of participants >60 years:**
- Remaining sample: 396 eyes (242 MDD, 154 control)
- Age difference: No longer significant (P=0.18)
- Primary findings remained consistent:
  - Mean macular thickness: MDD 273.25±16.09 vs Control 278.47±15.11 μm (P<0.001, d=-0.332)
  - Outer temporal thickness: P<0.001, d=-0.360
  - Cup-to-disc ratio: P=0.012, d=0.28

**2. Age-stratified analysis:**
| Age Group | MDD Eyes | Control Eyes | Mean Macular Thickness (μm) | P-value | Cohen's d |
|-----------|----------|--------------|----------------------------|---------|-----------|
| <35 years | 98 | 82 | 274.34±14.91 vs 279.50±15.13 | 0.003 | -0.342 |
| 35-50 years | 124 | 62 | 270.85±16.42 vs 277.18±14.85 | <0.001 | -0.405 |
| >50 years | 103 | 30 | 268.42±18.25 vs 276.35±16.82 | 0.028 | -0.452 |

Interaction P-value = 0.42 (no significant interaction between age group and MDD status)

**3. Age-matched subsample (18-58 years):**
- Matched sample: 226 MDD eyes, 154 control eyes
- Age: MDD 28.8±13.5 vs Control 26.4±11.7 years (P=0.416)
- Mean macular thickness: MDD 273.93±15.63 vs Control 278.47±15.11 μm (P=0.003, d=-0.294)

### Mixed-Effects Model Diagnostics

**Model specification:**
```
Y_ij = β₀ + β₁Group + β₂Age + β₃Sex + u_i + ε_ij

Random effects:
- σ²_u (between-participant variance): 45-65% of total variance
- σ²_ε (within-participant/residual variance): 35-55% of total variance
- ICC = σ²_u / (σ²_u + σ²_ε): 0.52-0.68

Model fit (for mean macular thickness):
- AIC (LMM): 3,247
- BIC (LMM): 3,271
- AIC (standard LM): 3,298 (ΔAIC = 51)
- BIC (standard LM): 3,315 (ΔBIC = 44)
```

**Assumption checking:**
- Residual normality: Shapiro-Wilk P=0.12 (acceptable)
- Homoscedasticity: Breusch-Pagan P=0.24 (acceptable)
- Multicollinearity: All VIF < 2.0 (no concern)
