## 1. 视网膜疾病 (Retinal Diseases)

### 1.1 年龄相关性黄斑变性 (Age-related Macular Degeneration, AMD)

#### 核心文献

| 文献 | 作者 | 期刊 | 年份 | 引用 | DOI |
|------|------|------|------|------|-----|
| Photodynamic Therapy of Subfoveal Choroidal Neovascularization in Age-related Macular Degeneration With Verteporfin | Neil M. Bressler | Archives of Ophthalmology | 1999 | 2186 | 10.1001/archopht.117.10.1329 |
| Guidelines for the management of neovascular age-related macular degeneration by the European Society of Retina Specialists (EURETINA) | Ursula Schmidt-Erfurth et al. | British Journal of Ophthalmology | 2014 | 632 | 10.1136/bjophthalmol-2014-305702 |
| Risk Factors for the Incidence of Advanced Age-Related Macular Degeneration in the Age-Related Eye Disease Study (AREDS) | Traci E Clemons et al. | Ophthalmology | 2005 | 678 | 10.1016/j.ophtha.2004.10.047 |

#### 关键知识点
- **抗VEGF治疗**: 雷珠单抗(ranibizumab)、贝伐单抗(bevacizumab)、阿柏西普(aflibercept)已成为nAMD的一线治疗
- **光动力疗法**: Verteporfin用于经典型CNV，但已被抗VEGF疗法取代为主流
- **风险因素**: 年龄、吸烟、家族史、基因变异(CFH, ARMS2)
- **AREDS研究**: 证实了抗氧化剂+锌的补充可延缓中重度AMD进展

#### 最新进展 (2024-2025)
- 延长给药间隔的治疗方案(treat-and-extend)
- 双特异性抗体(faricimab)的临床应用
- AI辅助诊断系统的准确性验证

### 1.2 糖尿病视网膜病变 (Diabetic Retinopathy, DR)

#### 核心文献

| 文献 | 作者 | 期刊 | 年份 | 引用 | DOI |
|------|------|------|------|------|-----|
| Development and Validation of a Deep Learning System for Diabetic Retinopathy and Related Eye Diseases | Daniel Shu Wei Ting et al. | JAMA | 2017 | 2215 | 10.1001/jama.2017.18152 |
| Diabetic Retinopathy Preferred Practice Pattern® | Christina J. Flaxel et al. | Ophthalmology | 2019 | 671 | 10.1016/j.ophtha.2019.09.025 |
| Epidemiology of diabetic retinopathy, diabetic macular edema and related vision loss | Ryan Lee et al. | Eye and Vision | 2015 | 1535 | 10.1186/s40662-015-0026-2 |
| The RESTORE Study | Paul Mitchell et al. | Ophthalmology | 2011 | 1315 | 10.1016/j.ophtha.2011.01.031 |

#### 关键知识点
- **流行病学**: 全球约2.85亿糖尿病患者，1/3有DR表现
- **深度学习应用**: AI系统在DR筛查中的敏感性和特异性均>90%
- **DME治疗**: 雷珠单抗单药或联合激光治疗优于传统激光
- **视力威胁性DR**: 包括严重非增殖性DR、增殖性DR、DME

#### 治疗指南要点
- 筛查: 1型糖尿病诊断后5年开始，2型糖尿病确诊时开始
- 抗VEGF: 一线治疗选择
- 全视网膜光凝: 高危增殖性DR

## 2. 青光眼 (Glaucoma)

### 2.1 原发性开角型青光眼 (Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma, POAG)

#### 核心文献

| 文献 | 作者 | 期刊 | 年份 | 引用 | DOI |
|------|------|------|------|------|-----|
| European Glaucoma Society Terminology and Guidelines for Glaucoma, 5th Edition | Augusto Azuara-Blanco et al. | British Journal of Ophthalmology | 2021 | 562 | 10.1136/bjophthalmol-2021-egsguidelines |
| Latanoprost for open-angle glaucoma (UKGTS) | David F. Garway-Heath et al. | The Lancet | 2014 | 643 | 10.1016/s0140-6736(14)62111-5 |
| Selective laser trabeculoplasty versus eye drops for first-line treatment of ocular hypertension and glaucoma (LiGHT) | Gus Gazzard et al. | The Lancet | 2019 | 530 | 10.1016/s0140-6736(18)32213-x |

#### 关键知识点
- **一线治疗选择**: 选择性激光小梁成形术(SLT)可作为一线治疗替代滴眼液
- **药物治疗**: 前列腺素类似物(拉坦前列素)为一线用药
- **危险因素**: 高眼压、年龄、种族(非洲裔)、家族史
- **目标眼压**: 根据基线损伤程度设定个体化目标

---

### 2.2 青光眼筛查与诊断

#### 核心文献

| 文献 | 作者 | 期刊 | 年份 | 引用 | DOI |
|------|------|------|------|------|-----|
| The clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of screening for open angle glaucoma | Jennifer Burr et al. | Health Technology Assessment | 2007 | 539 | 10.3310/hta11410 |

#### 诊断要点
- 结构性改变: 视神经乳头凹陷扩大、RNFL变薄
- 功能性改变: 视野缺损(旁中心暗点、弓形暗点)
- 影像学: OCT-GCC和RNFL分析
- 靶眼压设定: 轻度(<6dB)目标降低20-30%，重度(>12dB)目标降低40-50%
