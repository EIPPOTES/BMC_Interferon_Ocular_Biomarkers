# AAO临床指南与Preferred Practice Patterns

## 概述
美国眼科学会（American Academy of Ophthalmology, AAO）发布的Preferred Practice Patterns（PPP）是全球眼科临床实践的权威指南。

---

- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.ophtha.2008.09.032

### 2. 白内障PPP指南证据基础
- **文献**: Identification and Description of Reliable Evidence for 2016 American Academy of Ophthalmology Preferred Practice Pattern Guidelines for Cataract in the Adult Eye
- **作者**: Asieh Golozar, Chen Yu-jiang, Kristina Lindsley等
- **期刊**: JAMA Ophthalmology
- **年份**: 2018
- **DOI**: 10.1001/jamaophthalmol.2018.0786

- **URL**: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaophthalmology/articlepdf/2677569/jamaophthalmology_golozar_2018_oi_180015.pdf
- **分级**: A级
- **摘要要点**:
  - AAO自2016年起与Cochrane Eyes and Vision US Satellite合作更新指南
  - 指南需要可靠的系统性综述证据支持推荐意见
  - 建立了证据质量评估体系
