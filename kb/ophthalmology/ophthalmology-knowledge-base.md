## 青光眼

### 临床指南质量评估

**高质量指南评估研究**:  
- *A Critical Appraisal and Comparison of the Quality and Recommendations of Glaucoma Clinical Practice Guidelines* (2011)
- 作者: Yvonne Ou, Ivan Goldberg, C. Migdal 等
- **来源**: Ophthalmology
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.ophtha.2011.03.038
- **引用**: 55 citations

### 微创手术 (MIGS) 指南

**最新综述**:  
- *Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS) recommendations in Clinical Practice Guidelines for open angle glaucoma and MIGS procedures: A scoping review* (2024)
- **来源**: European Journal of Ophthalmology
- **DOI**: 10.1177/11206721241276223
- **引用**: 6 citations
- **核心发现**: 多项国际青光眼CPGs方法学质量高，但MIGS覆盖稀疏且不够具体

**系统性综述**:  
- *Systematic review of clinical practice guidelines for the diagnosis and management of open angle glaucoma* (2024-2025)
- **发现**: NICE、IETSI和SNSG指南方法学质量最高
- **来源**: Journal of Glaucoma, DOI: 10.1097/ijg.0000000000002642

### 青光眼药物治疗网络Meta分析

- *Network Meta-analysis for Clinical Practice Guidelines: A Case Study on First-Line Medical Therapies for Primary Open-Angle Glaucoma* (2016)
- **来源**: Annals of Internal Medicine
- **DOI**: 10.7326/m15-2367
- **引用**: 41 citations

### 儿童青光眼

- *A systematic review of clinical practice guidelines for childhood glaucoma* (2022)
- **来源**: BMJ Open Ophthalmology
- **DOI**: 10.1136/bmjophth-2021-000933
- **引用**: 25 citations
- **关键发现**: 目前没有专门针对儿童青光眼的高质量临床指南
